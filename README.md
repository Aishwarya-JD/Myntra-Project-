## 📌 Project Overview:

This project analyzes Myntra's product catalog to uncover insights related to pricing, discounts, product categories, customer ratings, and brand performance.

The dataset contains information about products such as:

- Product Name
- Brand Name
- Product Category
- Marked Price
- Discounted Price
- Ratings
- Rating Count
- Available Sizes
- Product Descriptions

The objective was to clean, transform, and analyze the data using Microsoft Excel and create meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards.

## 📈 Dashboard Insights:

### Pricing Analysis
- Compared marked prices and discounted prices.
- Identified products with the highest discounts.
- Analyzed price distribution across categories.

### Brand Analysis
- Top-performing brands based on ratings.
- Brands offering maximum discounts.
- Brand-wise product distribution.

### Category Analysis
- Most listed product categories.
- Highest-rated categories.
- Discount trends across categories.

### Customer Review Analysis
- Average rating by category.
- Most reviewed products.
- Relationship between ratings and sales popularity.

## 🎯 Business Questions Solved:

- Which brands offer the highest discounts?

- What categories receive the highest customer ratings?
